# SAMPLE DATA - fictional home visit instructions

These instructions are made up for the demo. They are not real procedures.

## Before a home visit

1. Confirm the visit time with the family one day ahead.
2. Tell the shift coordinator which address you are going to and when you expect to be back.
3. Always visit in pairs. Never go to a first visit alone.

## During the visit

1. Wear your volunteer badge where it can be seen.
2. Stay in the shared rooms of the home and keep the visit under one hour.
3. If anyone seems unwell or unsafe, leave politely and call the coordinator.

## After the visit

1. Log the visit on the contact's card the same day.
2. Note any needs the family mentioned, such as food, transport or paperwork.

## Cancelling a visit

1. If you cannot attend, phone the coordinator at least three hours before the visit.
2. The coordinator will tell the family and find a replacement volunteer.
3. Do not cancel by text message only.
